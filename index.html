WHO WE ARE

Building the Future of Aerospace Innovation

Eagle Crest Aerospace is an independent aerospace research and innovation organization committed to advancing science, engineering, and technology through research, innovation, education, and collaboration.

We believe every student and researcher has the potential to create meaningful technological innovations that contribute to the future of aerospace.

Research

Conducting multidisciplinary research in aerospace, propulsion, satellites, UAVs, avionics and AI.

Innovation

Transforming engineering ideas into practical technologies, patents and prototypes.

Education

Inspiring students through research, mentorship, workshops, technical training and innovation.

Our Mission

To inspire innovation, conduct high-quality aerospace research, mentor future engineers, and develop technologies that contribute to the advancement of aerospace engineering.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized independent aerospace research organization known for innovation, education, collaboration and engineering excellence.
